Narrative intelligence

Aevryn does not replace the story. It listens to it.

Evidence-backed canon

Accepted facts must trace back to the story. Unknown stays unknown, and Aevryn treats uncertainty as a valid result.

Timeline-aware state

Character state, world state, relationships, equipment, injuries, and scene context evolve as chapters move forward.

Production-ready context

Scene sheets, character cards, continuity reports, prompt packs, and exports are generated from structured story understanding.

IDE-style workspace

Built like a focused production tool.

Aevryn is organized around projects, imports, processed outputs, monitoring, settings, and exports. The app should feel like opening a creative workspace, not wandering through a generic dashboard.

Trust by design

Your stories belong to you.

Aevryn is designed around private manuscripts, metadata-only monitoring, evidence-backed outputs, and clear boundaries between storage, database, engine, API, and frontend.

No training on user stories without explicit opt-in Private manuscripts and generated exports Metadata-only support and monitoring posture Canon accepts evidence, not hidden assumptions

Public beta preparation

Aevryn is moving toward public beta deliberately.

The product is being hardened before outside users upload private work. Legal review, final public-beta signoff, and production readiness checks remain required before broad release.
