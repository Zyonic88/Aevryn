# Aevryn Terms Of Service

> Draft for attorney review before public launch.

These draft terms describe intended product boundaries for Aevryn. They are not
a substitute for legal review and are not approved for public beta.

---

# Parties And Service

Aevryn is operated by Aetherra Labs.

Aevryn helps users import, analyze, organize, and generate structured creative
production outputs from user-controlled story projects.

These draft terms are intended to govern access to the Aevryn platform,
including the web application, API, hosted services, documentation, and related
support channels.

---

# Eligibility

Users must be legally able to use Aevryn under applicable law.

Public launch must include final attorney-reviewed age, minor-use, and parental
consent language before Aevryn is offered to minors or users who may be treated
as minors in their jurisdiction.

---

# Acceptable Use

Users may use Aevryn for lawful creative, educational, research, and production
workflows.

Users may not:

* attack, disrupt, scan, overload, or bypass Aevryn systems
* access or attempt to access another user's account, project, manuscript,
  export, or private data
* upload or process content they do not have rights to use
* violate law or infringe intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, or other
  rights
* distribute malware, phishing content, spam, or abusive automation
* use Aevryn to evade security, access, rate-limit, or abuse-prevention controls
* use Aevryn for illegal material or activity

The detailed platform-use boundaries are tracked in
`docs/ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md`.

---

# User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

* having rights to upload, paste, import, process, export, and otherwise use
  their content in Aevryn
* maintaining account security
* reviewing outputs before publishing, relying on, or distributing them
* complying with applicable laws and third-party rights
* keeping private credentials, tokens, access links, and unpublished manuscripts
  out of support tickets unless Aetherra Labs explicitly requests a narrowly
  scoped diagnostic sample

---

# Accounts

Users must provide accurate account information where required.

Aetherra Labs may limit, suspend, or terminate accounts that violate these
terms, create security risk, create legal risk, evade enforcement, or abuse the
platform.

Aetherra Labs should provide notice where practical, but may act without prior
notice when needed for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, abuse
prevention, or protection of other users.

---

# Payments

Payments are future scope.

Future paid plans must define billing, renewal, cancellation, refund, credits,
tax, chargeback, and nonpayment terms before paid plans launch.

No paid-plan language in this draft should be treated as final until reviewed by
counsel and implemented in the production billing system.

---

# Intellectual Property

Users retain ownership of uploaded stories, pasted story text, imported source
files, project data, generated Canon, generated exports, and user-generated
production outputs.

Aetherra Labs does not claim ownership of user stories or user project Canon.

Users grant Aetherra Labs a limited license to host, process, transmit, display,
analyze, and export user content only as needed to operate Aevryn for the user's
account, provide support requested by the user, maintain security, comply with
law, and enforce these terms.

Aetherra Labs retains ownership of Aevryn software, platform code, product
design, trademarks, service infrastructure, documentation, and non-user-created
system materials.

---

# AI And Canon Boundaries

Aevryn may use AI-assisted extraction or generation when enabled for a project
or product workflow.

Provider output is not Canon by itself. Aevryn validates provider output against
story evidence before accepting it into project state.

Aetherra Labs does not train models on uploaded, pasted, imported, or generated
user story/project content by default. Any future training or improvement use of
user story/project content must be explicit, opt-in, and disclosed.

Final provider disclosure language is tracked in
`docs/AEVRYN_AI_PROVIDER_DISCLOSURE_DECISION.md` and still requires attorney
review before public beta.

---

# Copyright And Repeat Infringer Draft

Users must not upload, import, process, export, or distribute content through
Aevryn unless they have the legal right to do so.

Users may report copyright or rights concerns to:

```text
abuse@aevryn.ai
```

Aetherra Labs may remove or restrict access to allegedly infringing content,
suspend accounts involved in repeat infringement, preserve relevant metadata
where legally required, and terminate accounts where appropriate.

Final DMCA, repeat-infringer, counter-notice, agent, and copyright-takedown
language must be drafted or approved by counsel before public launch.

---

# Service Availability

Aevryn may change, pause, limit, or discontinue features.

Aetherra Labs should make reasonable efforts to preserve user access and data
export paths, but no service can guarantee uninterrupted availability.

Public launch should define any maintenance, incident, support, export, or
service-availability commitments that Aetherra Labs is willing to make.

---

# Termination

Users may stop using Aevryn at any time.

Aetherra Labs may limit, suspend, or terminate access for violations, legal
obligations, security threats, abuse, fraud, nonpayment once payments exist, or
risk to Aevryn or other users.

After termination, Aevryn may delete active product data according to the
published privacy, backup, and retention policies. Some records may remain for
security, audit, abuse prevention, legal compliance, billing, or disaster
recovery where disclosed and legally permitted.

---

Aevryn is provided as-is unless a final attorney-reviewed agreement says
otherwise.

replace professional legal, financial, medical, editorial, publishing, or
rights-clearance advice.

Final warranty disclaimer wording, conspicuousness, formatting, and enforceable
scope must be reviewed by counsel before public launch.
