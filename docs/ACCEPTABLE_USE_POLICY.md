# Aevryn Acceptable Use Policy

> Draft for attorney review before public launch.

This policy separates product safety from content ownership. It is not a
substitute for legal review and is not approved for public beta.

---

# Core Rule

```text
Aevryn is content-aware, not content-opinionated.
```

Aevryn may support lawful creative work, including mature fiction, while still
prohibiting unlawful content, platform abuse, rights violations, and security
abuse.

---

# Allowed Uses

Aevryn may be used for lawful work such as:

* fiction
* novels
* web novels
* screenplays
* comics
* mature novels
* academic work
* private worldbuilding
* continuity tracking
* prompt-pack preparation
* exports and production planning

Users must have the rights needed to upload, paste, import, process, export, or
otherwise use their content in Aevryn.

---

# Prohibited Uses

Users may not use Aevryn for:

* copyright infringement
* uploading, importing, processing, or exporting content they do not have rights
  to use
* malware, credential theft, phishing, spam, bot activity, or deceptive
  automation
* illegal material or illegal activity
* doxxing, stalking, harassment campaigns, or targeted abuse
* attempts to access another user's account, projects, source files, exports, or
  private data
* attempts to attack, scan, scrape, overload, benchmark-abuse, reverse engineer,
  or bypass Aevryn systems
* attempts to evade rate limits, access controls, content controls, billing
  controls once payments exist, or security monitoring
* attempts to extract secrets, tokens, credentials, private storage references,
  private URLs, or internal infrastructure details
* interference with monitoring, logging, audit records, abuse detection, or
  incident response
* using support, privacy, security, or abuse channels to submit spam, threats,
  malware, credentials, or unnecessary full manuscripts

---

# Platform Abuse

In this policy, platform abuse means behavior that harms, disrupts, exploits,
evades, or misuses Aevryn, Aetherra Labs, other users, provider services, or
related infrastructure.

Examples include:

* creating accounts to evade suspension or enforcement
* intentionally submitting repeated failing jobs to degrade service
* using imports, prompts, exports, or support channels as spam or payload
  delivery systems
* attempting to bypass authorization or read another user's project data
* using automation in a way that degrades availability or evades published
  limits
* probing for secrets, private object-storage references, signed URLs, or
  non-public endpoints
* abusing beta limitations, unsupported workflows, or known bugs after being
  warned

---

# Mature And Explicit Fiction

Aevryn may support lawful mature or explicit fictional content when the user has
the rights to process it and the content is not otherwise prohibited.

For V2 public beta, Aevryn is 18+ only.

Mature and explicit projects may be subject to:

* project content classification
* UI warnings
* provider restrictions
* export restrictions
* support-routing limits
* abuse review
* future moderation or account controls

Aevryn does not approve unlawful material merely because it is fictional.

Explicit sexual content is restricted content for V2 public beta. Aevryn may
classify that an import appears restricted, but restricted explicit sexual
content must not be processed, summarized, extracted, converted into prompt
packs, or exposed as detailed structured output unless attorney review, provider
policy review, and an approved adult-content gate are complete.

Attorney review must approve final public wording for age restrictions,
minor-protection requirements, explicit-content boundaries, and any legally
required moderation or reporting duties before public beta.

---

# Copyright, Rights, And Repeat Infringers

Users must not use Aevryn to infringe copyrights, trademarks, privacy rights,
publicity rights, contractual rights, or other third-party rights.

Rights or copyright concerns should be reported to:

```text
abuse@aevryn.ai
```

Aetherra Labs may remove or restrict allegedly infringing content, preserve
metadata where legally required, suspend accounts, and terminate repeat
infringers where appropriate.

Final DMCA, counter-notice, repeat-infringer, agent, and takedown language must
be drafted or approved by counsel before public launch.

---

# Security Research

Good-faith security research must follow `docs/SECURITY_DISCLOSURE.md`.

Security research is not allowed to access another user's data, alter or delete
data, exfiltrate secrets, degrade availability, or publicly disclose a
vulnerability before remediation coordination.

---

# Enforcement

Aetherra Labs may take action when it believes this policy has been violated or
when action is needed to protect users, Aevryn, providers, infrastructure, legal
compliance, or platform integrity.

Possible actions include:

* warning the user
* limiting features, imports, exports, provider-backed extraction, or support
  access
* removing or restricting content
* suspending or terminating accounts
* blocking abusive traffic or automation
* preserving metadata for security, abuse, legal, or audit purposes
* reporting activity to providers, infrastructure partners, or authorities where
  legally required or appropriate

Aetherra Labs should provide notice where practical, but may act without prior
notice for urgent security, legal, abuse, fraud, or user-protection reasons.

Attorney review must approve final enforcement, appeal, notice, and repeat
violation language before public launch.

---

# Appeals And Mistakes

Users may contact Aetherra Labs if they believe enforcement was mistaken.

Target contact:

```text
support@aevryn.ai
```

Privacy, security, rights, or abuse-sensitive appeals may be routed to the
appropriate contact alias.

Before public launch, Aetherra Labs must decide which enforcement actions are
appealable, what information users should provide, and what response timeline is
practical and legally appropriate.
